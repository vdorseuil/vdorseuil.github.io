I am a second-year PhD student in the [DATA Team](https://www.di.ens.fr/data/) at [École Normale Supérieure (ENS) - PSL](https://www.ens.psl.eu/), under the supervision of [Olivier Cappé](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cappe/) and Jamal Atif. My research focuses on Machine Learning, specifically Differential Privacy and Membership Inference Attacks.

During the summer of 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) (affiliated with the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/)), under the supervision of [Nicolas Papernot](https://www.papernot.fr/), in the [Clever Hans Lab](https://cleverhans.io/).

Before joining ENS, I graduated from [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) (Cycle Ingénieur, X2021), where I majored in Applied Mathematics with a minor in Computer Science. I also hold a Master's degree in Machine Learning from [Université Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/).
