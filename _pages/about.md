I am a second-year PhD student in the [DATA Team](https://www.di.ens.fr/data/) at the [Computer Science Department (DI ENS)](https://www.di.ens.fr/) of [École Normale Supérieure (ENS) - PSL](https://www.ens.psl.eu/), under the supervision of [Olivier Cappé](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cappe/) and [Jamal Atif](https://dblp.org/pid/98/6766.html). My research focuses on Machine Learning, specifically Differential Privacy and Membership Inference Attacks.

In Summer 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) and the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), hosted by [Nicolas Papernot](https://www.papernot.fr/) in the [CleverHans Lab](https://cleverhans.io/).

Before joining ENS, I graduated from [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) (Cycle Ingénieur, X2021), where I majored in Applied Mathematics with a minor in Computer Science. I also hold a Master's degree in Machine Learning from [Université Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/).

I am always happy to discuss research or potential collaborations, so feel free to reach out!
