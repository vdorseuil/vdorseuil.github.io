Our new preprint, *Assessing Per-Sample Membership Inference Vulnerability without Retraining*, is now live on arXiv. The paper presents an efficient approach to evaluate per-sample membership inference attack (MIA) vulnerability while bypassing the overhead of retraining shadow models.
