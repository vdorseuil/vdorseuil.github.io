**[Preprint]:** Our new preprint, *Detectability without Interference: Efficient Canary Design for Privacy Auditing in One Run*, is live on arXiv. It frames canary optimization as a bi-level problem and introduces IBIS, a framework that efficiently crafts multiple canaries for privacy audits in one run.

You can check it out [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27292)!📄
